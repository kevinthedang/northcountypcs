What I work on

Desktops and laptops, home and small business. Builds, upgrades, and clean installs done carefully — plus a straight answer on whether the machine is worth it.

  • Custom PC builds

    Gaming rigs, workstations, and budget office machines specced to what you actually do — assembled, cable-managed, and stress-tested before pickup.

  • Upgrades

    SSD and RAM upgrades, GPU installs, PSU replacements, cooling fixes. Usually the cheapest way to make an older machine feel new again.

  • Tune-ups & installs

    Dust and thermal paste, a clean Windows install, drivers, and moving your files to a new drive. The difference is usually dramatic.

  • Diagnostics & advice

    Not sure if it's worth upgrading or replacing? Bring it in for a look. I'll tell you what I think is wrong, what I can fix, and what's beyond me.

Board-level repair and data recovery from failed drives I refer out to specialists — those are jobs you only get one shot at.

Repair first

I'd rather save your machine than sell you a new one.

There's already too much e-waste in the world. If a new SSD buys your laptop another three years, that's the answer you'll get — even though a new build is the bigger sale. When replacement really is the right call, I'll tell you straight, but I won't invent a reason to sell you one.

I'm self-taught, building this up one machine at a time. So I'm careful, I check my work twice, and if a job is outside what I can do well, I'll tell you — I won't learn on your machine.

What the full diagnostic gets you

  • A confirmed fault, not a guess. I reproduce the problem instead of swapping parts and hoping something sticks.
  • I test before you buy parts. Temperatures, memory, and drive health get checked so you're not paying for a replacement that isn't the problem.
  • Written findings and a firm quote. Likely cause, recommended fix, parts and labor, and anything I couldn't verify.
  • Your data left alone. I only look at what's needed to diagnose the fault, and I'll ask before copying anything.

How it works

Four steps, no runaround.

  1. Tell me what's wrong

    Call or email with the machine and the symptom. A photo or error message helps.

  2. Drop off or book on-site

    Drop off in Oceanside by appointment, or have me come to you anywhere in North County.

  3. Approve the quote

    Once I know what's wrong you get a firm price. Nothing gets replaced or repaired until you approve it.

  4. Pick up a working machine

    Everything is tested and verified before it goes back to you.
